# Engineering displays

Reviewed 2026-11-20.

## Cockpit MFD or engineering terminal

Cockpit multifunction displays and dedicated engineering stations offer different views of ship systems. At a crew station, identify the selected page and room or component before interpreting an alert. The 3D schematic can show controls, filters and status icons together. [CIG Engineering Gameplay Guide](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/20935-Engineering-Gameplay-Guide) · [Star Citizen Wiki — Engineering](https://starcitizen.tools/Engineering)

## Life support load and room oxygen

Game build: 4.10.3

The engineering life-support percentage describes load against the system's rated capacity. Reheating cold air after venting affects that load. Look for a separately labeled oxygen reading to report the atmosphere in a particular room. [CIG 4.10.3 release notes](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/Patch-Notes/21429-Star-Citizen-Alpha-4103) · [Community patch reference](https://starcitizen.tools/index.php?title=Star_Citizen_Alpha_4.10.3&oldid=411888#Ships_and_vehicles)

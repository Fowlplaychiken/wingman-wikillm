# Missions, survival and exploration

Reviewed 2026-11-14.

## Contracts, reputation and CrimeStat

Use the active contract in mobiGlas for the issuer, objectives, progress and destination. Reputation can affect which contracts become available. Crime rating depends on the action and jurisdiction. Check the current rating and location before using advice about fines, landing restrictions or prison. [RSI Missions and Contracts](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008918933-Missions-and-Contracts) · [RSI Crime Rating](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008162913-Crime-Rating)

## Combat, rescue and regeneration

Arena Commander offers standalone combat practice. Ship combat and countermeasures depend on the active seat, loadout and control profile. In medical gameplay, use the suit, medical tool or available medbed for the patient's actual indicators. Blood drug level is one of the monitored values. Regeneration is the in-game respawn process. [RSI Arena Commander](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003091714-Arena-Commander) · [RSI Healing and Medical Treatment](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409996816535-Healing-and-Medical-Treatment)

## Exploration, racing and lore

Galactapedia covers in-universe locations, species, manufacturers, history and spacecraft. The community wiki covers lore alongside game mechanics. Use the current starmap to verify whether a lore destination is accessible. Use current event or mode information for racing. [CIG Galactapedia](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/galactapedia) · [Community wiki](https://starcitizen.tools/) · [RSI Daily Life](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/16444523494423-Daily-Life-in-the-Verse)

## LIVE, PTU and patch troubleshooting

Record the game build and whether it was LIVE or PTU when comparing behavior. Check current service status, then the matching known-issues page. Use Issue Council for reproducible game problems with the correct channel and actual steps. [RSI PTU FAQ](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013195927-Public-Test-Universe-PTU-FAQ) · [RSI Status](https://status.robertsspaceindustries.com/) · [RSI Issue Council guide](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/115009643527-Bug-Reports-Using-the-Issue-Council)

# Environmental indicators

Reviewed 2026-11-20.

## On foot and in EVA

Heat and cold affect the character as well as the surroundings. The suit's protection and exposure time matter. Read the label before reporting a temperature. The outside temperature alone cannot establish hypothermia or heat injury. Hunger and hydration are separate survival needs. [CIG actor-status postmortem](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/17671-Invictus-Launch-Week-Alpha-39X-Postmortem)

Radiation exposure depends on the surroundings and protection. An environmental reading describes the location. An accumulated dose describes the character. Keep the displayed unit with the value and distinguish a suit protection rating from either reading. A radiation sign identifies a hazard area, and a patient's condition needs its own evidence. [Community radiation reference](https://starcitizen.tools/Radiation) · [Alpha 4.2 release notes](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/Patch-Notes/20638-Star-Citizen-Alpha-42)

Use suit oxygen, blood oxygen saturation, injuries and blood drug level only for the subject and instrument shown. In a rescue, the medical display can describe another player. A medical reading alone cannot establish the amount of oxygen left in the player's suit. [RSI medical treatment](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409996816535-Healing-and-Medical-Treatment)

## Surface travel and ground vehicles

Aberdeen's sulfurous haze reduces visibility on the ground. A rover crew can use that location information when planning a route. The atmosphere outside does not establish the air inside a particular cabin, the crew's oxygen supply or a vehicle fault. Check the vehicle display and the character's own instruments for those. [CIG Galactapedia — Aberdeen](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/galactapedia/article/0qanJarxqa-aberdeen-stanton-1b) · [Star Citizen Wiki — Aberdeen](https://starcitizen.tools/Aberdeen)

Cold, wind and breathable air vary independently. For example, CIG describes Calliope as cold and windy. Magda has areas with manageable temperatures but an atmosphere a human cannot breathe. A location description supports planning. A current weather alert needs current evidence. [Galactapedia — Calliope](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/galactapedia/article/Rz2PaqwwPM-calliope-stanton-4a) · [Galactapedia — Magda](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/galactapedia/article/0qanJYxAG7-magda-stanton-1c)

## Ship and crew engineering displays

Game build: 4.10.3

On a ship engineering display, life-support load measures work against the system's rated capacity. It can exceed 100 percent while reheating atmosphere after venting. Use the selected room's pressure, temperature and oxygen labels for conditions in that room. The ship-wide load is a different measurement from suit oxygen or a room's gas mix. [CIG 4.10.3 release notes](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/Patch-Notes/21429-Star-Citizen-Alpha-4103) · [Community patch reference](https://starcitizen.tools/index.php?title=Star_Citizen_Alpha_4.10.3&oldid=411888#Ships_and_vehicles)

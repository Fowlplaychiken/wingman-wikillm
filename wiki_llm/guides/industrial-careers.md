# Industrial careers

Reviewed 2026-11-14.

## Mining, extraction and refining

Scan a deposit for its composition and properties. During fracture, build charge and keep it in the indicated optimal band. Switch to extraction for collectable fragments. Plan whether to refine, sell or use the material and check the actual refinery job at the terminal. [RSI Mining Basics](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007611573-Mining-Basics) · [RSI Daily Life](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/16444523494423-Daily-Life-in-the-Verse)

## Salvage, blueprints and fabrication

Hull scraping collects recycled material composite, or RMC. Structural salvage is a separate operation and its recovered material can need refining. Cargo and components on a wreck are separate recovery opportunities. Item fabrication uses a blueprint and its specified materials. Read the current blueprint and terminal for required quantities and compatible material grades. [Community salvage guide](https://starcitizen.tools/Salvage) · [RSI Daily Life](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/16444523494423-Daily-Life-in-the-Verse)

## Cargo, hauling and commodity trade

A hauling contract specifies pickup and delivery. Commodity trading uses the player's funds and current offers. At a freight elevator, check the intended warehouse, goods and sizes before loading. Nominal SCU capacity is only one part of fit. Door clearance, box arrangement and access also matter. Use a current terminal or quote for price and stock. [RSI Cargo Guide](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/20040-Cargo-Guide) · [Community hauling reference](https://starcitizen.tools/Hauling)

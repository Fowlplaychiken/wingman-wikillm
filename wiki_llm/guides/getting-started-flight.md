# Getting started and flight

Reviewed 2026-11-14.

## First session, hangars and ASOP

Your first primary residence determines where your starting equipment and ships are stored and where your personal hangar is. At the spaceport, use an ASOP vehicle terminal to retrieve your ship. Read the assigned hangar before taking the elevator. A port away from home can assign a temporary hangar. [RSI Getting Started](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025028633-Getting-Started-in-the-Verse) · [RSI Hangar FAQs](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013325128-Hangar-FAQs)

## Keybindings, equipment and personal inventory

Look up the active device profile in Advanced Controls Customization or use a current imported binding in Wingman. Key and button assignments vary by profile. Personal inventory is what you carry. Local inventory belongs to the selected location and is accessible through a storage kiosk. Equipment upgrades also depend on the current ship, exact variant and available items. [RSI keybinding guide](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000134267-Set-up-keybindings-for-your-peripherals) · [RSI Personal Inventory](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409996738327-Personal-Inventory) · [RSI Ship Matrix](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/ship-matrix)

## Quantum travel and navigation

Choose a destination in mobiGlas Maps and set a route. The route panel displays planned jumps and estimated quantum fuel. Select the navigation master mode and its quantum operating mode, align with the destination marker and let the drive calibrate before engaging. For an actual route, check fuel and access at the destination with fresh evidence. [RSI Quantum Travel](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019449994-How-to-Quantum-Travel)
